// process

How working together goes

A simple loop that works for everything from a one-off script to a multi-week tool. No spec-writing marathons, no surprise scope.

  1. 01

    The problem

    A few lines about what’s manual, broken or missing. No spec, no jargon required. A description of the pain is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Scope & proposal

    I check feasibility and send an honest proposal: approach, deliverables and a timeline we both agree on. If it doesn’t fit, you get a straight answer on why not.

  3. 03

    Build

    I build in the open: working code at each stage, decisions explained as they’re made, and a running version you can look at, not just hear about.

  4. 04

    Verify & hand over

    We test the result in your environment, and you get a project you can run yourself, with whatever it needs to be understood and maintained.

  5. 05

    Follow-up

    After delivery I stay available for fixes and small adjustments. No disappearing acts once the invoice is paid.
